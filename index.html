🌸 ~ About Me ~

» Intro

Immersive developer, storyteller & creative problem-solver 💜

Hi! I'm Lea Bogovic, a VR & game developer who loves building experiences that feel alive, emotional, and immersive. I'm completing my Bachelor’s Honours Degree in Software Design & Game Development (VR) at TUS and currently working as an Immersive Developer with Mersus Technologies.

I enjoy working across both game development and web, from VR hand-tracking and physics systems to building cozy, interactive websites. My favourite languages right now are C# and HTML, and I love using them to turn ideas into spaces people can actually step into.

As a developer, I love solving interaction problems, especially in VR. Things like “how should this feel in someone’s hands?” or “what feedback tells the player they did the right thing?” are the kind of questions I enjoy turning into code.

Currently: polishing tiny interactions and overthinking particles.

Theme: cozy VR dev with a hint of fantasy and gamer brain.

🌿 What drives my work

  • I love storytelling, not just through dialogue, but through environments, pacing, and the way interactions feel.
  • I’m obsessed with immersion. As someone who gets completely absorbed in films, games, and projects, I want my work to make people feel that same pull.
  • I care about experiences that feel intuitive, playful, and emotionally meaningful, whether it’s a VR training module or a personal project.

🛠️ How I work

I like mixing structure with curiosity,exploring ideas, testing them, and polishing the ones that feel right.

Right now: focused, curious, and refining small interaction details.

  • I build small prototypes to test ideas, interactions, physics, timing, sound, and feel. I’d rather try things quickly than guess how they’ll feel.
  • I use notes, screenshots, and short clips to track progress and decisions. It makes it easier to explain choices to others and to see how an idea evolved.
  • I like breaking work into tiny, finishable tasks so that big features feel less scary. Little wins keep the momentum going.
  • I enjoy collaborating in Agile teams, planning sprints, sharing progress, and adjusting when things change, but I’m also happy owning a whole feature or experience when needed.
  • I care a lot about feedback & polish, animation curves, particles, sound cues, and tiny timing tweaks that make an experience feel intentional and alive.
